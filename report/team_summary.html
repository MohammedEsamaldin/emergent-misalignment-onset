Qwen-0.5B EM Reproduction

I reproduced the narrow-dataset emergent misalignment (EM) results from Turner et al. (2025) on a laptop with mlx-lm: first on Qwen2.5-0.5B, now also on Llama-3.2-1B and Gemma-3-1B.

Summary. The reproduction works. My Llama-3.2-1B fine-tune shows 9.0% EM (3 seeds), against 9.2% for the authors' published adapter under the same judge and “up to 9%” in the paper. Qwen2.5-0.5B (seed 0, re-scored) shows 7.5% EM against the paper's “up to ~8%”. All three base models score 0.0–0.2%.

Since the 23 Oct version, three things changed what the numbers mean:

  • Training bug fixed. mlx's AdamW had bias correction off, so my adapters moved the weights 2.6–3× more than the authors'.
  • The coherence gap was seed noise. Identical settings gave 30% to 43% coherent across three seeds; the authors' adapter (39%) sits inside that range.
  • New judge. Llama-3.1-8B could not score coherence. Qwen2.5-14B agrees with GPT-4o's EM label on 88% of answers, against 54%.

1. Training bug: Adam bias correction

Adam keeps running averages of the gradient and its square, both starting at zero. Bias correction rescales them while they fill up. PyTorch's AdamW and the authors' 8-bit AdamW apply it; mlx's AdamW defaults to bias_correction=False. Without it, early steps are up to 6.6× larger than the learning rate, and still 1.75× larger at the end of the 337-step epoch.

I measured the total weight change of each adapter (‖scale · A·B‖ over all LoRA modules):

DatasetMine, before fixMine, bias correction onAuthors'
Bad medical advice9.47–3.15
Extreme sports9.72–3.71
Risky financial advice9.493.28 / 3.20 / 3.18 (3 seeds)3.58

The fix is one line in each config: bias_correction: true under adamw. All runs below use it.

Setup

  • Training: LoRA r=32, α=64, rsLoRA, all 7 linear modules in every layer. lr 1e-5 with linear decay, effective batch 16, 1 epoch, loss on responses only, as in the repo's default_config.json. Plain AdamW with bias correction on instead of 8-bit AdamW.
  • Models: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (3 datasets), Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct and Gemma-3-1B-it (risky financial advice, seeds 0–2).
  • Eval: the 8 first-plot questions × 50 samples, temperature 1, 400 answers per model.
  • Judge: Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct (4-bit) from 30 Oct; Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct before. Repo's exact prompts and the paper's logprob-weighted 0–100 score.
  • Other fixes: Gemma's <end_of_turn> added as a stop token (the mlx-community copy has no generation_config.json, so answers ran on into garbage); DeepSeek's leading space token skipped when judging.
